Can analysis be translated into something usable and testable?

Measure

The COM5508 group project collected RED content around Hong Kong food. The retained dataset contains topic, title, content and captured likes, saves, comments and shares. The presentation describes text cleaning, duplicate removal and sorting by likes; high-like and high-save examples were selected for prompting.

The captured counts have no exposure denominator. They cannot establish an engagement rate or show that a particular headline style caused better performance. Repeated post IDs across topic captures also require care when defining unique posts.

Translate

Content–headline examples informed few-shot prompts. Instructions focused on relevance, brevity and readable language. Feedback called out irrelevant tags, repeated source wording and incorrect restaurant counts.

Build

The retained notebook configures five draft titles, with three selected to request emoji. The Flask application connects a content-input form to title generation. This is prompt iteration and application prototyping, not model fine-tuning.

The historical prototype requested GPT-3.5 through a third-party provider; this portfolio documents its implementation, not a currently running API service.

Next decision

The original deck proposes online A/B testing of model versions or parameter settings under “Further Improvement”. No verified participant count, random-assignment record, exposure log or measured uplift is presented.

The practical next step would be to evaluate factuality and relevance before any online comparison. That is a future recommendation, not a completed experiment.

Contribution

Originally completed as a CityU COM5508 group project. I was responsible for the data work after collection, including cleaning and analysis, and for the generator. Data collection was handled by other group members. The group presentation remains a shared deliverable.

What this demonstrates

Connecting content data to a usable prototype, translating examples into prompt constraints, reviewing factual errors and separating a proposed experiment from measured results.
