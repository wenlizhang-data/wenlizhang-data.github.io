# Provenance and original-versus-rebuilt evidence

## Ownership and sources

The project originated as a City University of Hong Kong COM5507 group research project. The owner completed the data-related work; the original written report was a group deliverable. The 2026 refactoring and assisted review are separately identified.

Seven original per-media CSV captures supply 40,187 analytical input rows. The public source manifest records snapshot filenames, sizes and SHA-256 hashes; original source paths and comment-level snapshots remain private. The original university files were not overwritten.

## Historical versions

The legacy raw workbook and two cleaned/report exports were reconciled as far as retained evidence permits. `legacy_version_comparison.csv` contains historical version checks, not current sentiment results. Complete transformation lineage is unavailable; equal totals do not establish identical row-level classifications.

The owner supplied the Shimo sentiment text and screenshot after the initial browser access attempt failed. Its seven source totals agree with the historical report-version export. The screenshot's AL Neutral cell conflicts with itsbody text, pie-chart percentage and overall category total; the body value is internally consistent. The conflicting table cell was not used in current analysis. These materials are historical evidence, not a new analytical baseline; no claim is made that the Shimo website itself was accessed successfully.

## Original methods and limits of recovery

Recovered notebooks support TextBlob sentiment, frequency processing and inherited phrase rules; cleaning attempts and an incomplete Selenium collection draft were also retained privately. A bounded search did not recover runnable LSA or FastText implementations. Neither method is part of this rebuilt analysis. No new classifier or semantic topic model was introduced.

## 2026 changes

The canonical archive, common English gate, repaired per-comment phrase matching, explicit population denominators, video concentration, sensitivity checks and reusable visual system are documented portfolio improvements. Historical outputs are not relabelled as current results or used to claim model accuracy, business uplift or an improved audience response.
